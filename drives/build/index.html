How we use your location

Only if you press “Use my location”, and your browser asks first. The position stays in this page to pick nearby stops. It is never stored, never sent to us or anyone else, and never put in a link: the Maps buttons carry the stops only, and your maps app works out the way from where you are. Town, ZIP, distillery, and place names are matched against lists on this page.

Don’t use this page while driving. Archaeological sites, caves and private land are never suggested; stay on public property and respect posted signs.
