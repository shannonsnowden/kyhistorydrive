Privacy

Kentucky History Drive is a static site about Kentucky history. Story and map pages do not ask you to create an account. When advertising is turned on, Google AdSense may set cookies and use similar identifiers to show and measure ads.

Advertising cookies

Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads, including personalized ads based on pages you have visited, and to measure how those ads perform. That can happen on this site only when an ad slot is actually filled.

Google describes this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Details on advertising cookies are in How Google uses cookies in advertising.

Opting out of personalized ads

You can turn off ad personalization in Google Ads Settings. That choice is tied to the browser or Google account you use there. Turning it off does not stop ads from appearing; it asks Google to show ads that are not based on your interests.

Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, and US state messages

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalized ads. Visitors in US states that require it are shown a state privacy message. Both messages come from AdSense Privacy & messaging, which is Google’s certified consent-management platform for those regions. This site does not run a separate consent banner.

Other requests

Opening the map loads tiles from CARTO (OpenStreetMap data). The site stores your light or dark theme choice in local storage on this browser. Those requests are separate from advertising cookies.
