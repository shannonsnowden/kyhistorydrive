Privacy

Kentucky History Drive is a static site about Kentucky history. Story and map pages do not ask you to create an account. The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are opened. When advertising is turned on, Google AdSense may set cookies and use similar identifiers to show and measure ads.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-83G4SBQKT1) records how the site is used. That includes the page address, the page title, and general browser and device information such as language and screen size. On the main page, the map and individual stories use a hash address (for example /#map or /#timeline/a-story). A page view is sent when the page loads and again when that hash changes, so a story or the map counts separately from the home page. Google uses the IP address to derive an approximate location. Google Analytics 4 does not store the full IP address. When analytics cookies are allowed, a first-party cookie can recognize a later visit in the same browser. This site does not use Analytics to create an account.

Before the tag is configured, each page sets Google Consent Mode defaults. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization, and analytics storage start denied. The consent message for those regions is AdSense Privacy & messaging, loaded by the Google ad tag already in the page head. That message updates the consent choice itself. The tag waits briefly so a saved choice can apply before a page view is sent. If you do not consent, analytics cookies are not set. Google may still receive a cookieless ping that does not include those cookies. In other regions those storage types start granted. Where a US state requires a privacy message, that message also comes from AdSense Privacy & messaging.

You can opt out of Google Analytics in this browser with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. How Google handles information collected by its products is described in Google’s privacy policy.

Advertising cookies

Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads, including personalized ads based on pages you have visited, and to measure how those ads perform. That can happen on this site only when an ad slot is actually filled.

Google describes this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Details on advertising cookies are in How Google uses cookies in advertising.

Opting out of personalized ads

You can turn off ad personalization in Google Ads Settings. That choice is tied to the browser or Google account you use there. Turning it off does not stop ads from appearing; it asks Google to show ads that are not based on your interests.

Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, and US state messages

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalized ads or analytics cookies. Visitors in US states that require it are shown a state privacy message. Both messages come from AdSense Privacy & messaging, which is Google’s certified consent-management platform for those regions. The message updates Google Consent Mode on the page. This site does not run a separate consent banner.

Other requests

Opening the map loads tiles from CARTO (OpenStreetMap data). The site stores your light or dark theme choice in local storage on this browser. Those requests are separate from advertising cookies.
