    Layers

    How to use this map

    Start with the Kentucky Historical Society’s highway markers, then turn on more layers to see what else happened nearby.

    • Add history sites, national sites, Civil War and frontier war sites, museums, covered bridges, industry and mills, historic newspapers, parks, cemeteries, distilleries (mostly Bourbon), caves, and good places to eat.
    • Tap a pin to read its marker text or story.
    • Lay archaeological cultures and Native American territories under today’s markers. Both are approximate areas. “Show only” opens one nation’s map, most with a year slider.
    • Public caves are pinned. Other caves appear only by county, to protect them.
    • It runs in your phone’s browser, so it can ride along on a road trip.

    Year/era filters from Timeline also apply to year-aware layers here; highway Markers stay as layer toggles.
