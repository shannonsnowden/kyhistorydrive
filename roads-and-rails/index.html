Railroads

Lexington & Ohio Railroad
Kentucky's first railroad. Horse-drawn cars reached Frankfort from Lexington on 31 January 1834; the first steam run followed in January 1835. The line was chartered to carry Bluegrass goods toward the Ohio River.
Louisville & Frankfort Railroad
Closed the gap from Frankfort to the Ohio River at Louisville, with through service in 1851 after the Kentucky River bridge was finished.
Louisville & Nashville main line
Opened south from Louisville in stages from 1855, climbing Muldraugh's Hill to reach Elizabethtown in June 1858. Track met at Bowling Green on 10 August 1859, and the first through train to Nashville ran on 27 October 1859.
Cincinnati Southern Railway
Built by the city of Cincinnati for a direct route to Southern markets that bypassed Louisville and the L&N. It was open to Somerset by 23 July 1877, crossing the Kentucky River on High Bridge, and through service to Chattanooga began in 1880.

Parkways

Mountain Parkway
Winchester to Campton opened 14 January 1963, and Campton to Salyersville was dedicated 1 November 1963. It was built to open up eastern Kentucky.
Western Kentucky Parkway
Elizabethtown to near Eddyville opened in 1963, and the extension to I-24 at Eddyville followed in 1968.
Bluegrass Parkway
Opened 27 October 1965 between Elizabethtown and the Bluegrass at Versailles.
Purchase Parkway
Opened in 1966 (provisional) through the Jackson Purchase, from Calvert City to Fulton.
Pennyrile Parkway
Opened in 1969 (provisional) between Henderson and Hopkinsville.
Audubon Parkway
Opened 18 December 1970 between Henderson and Owensboro.
Daniel Boone (Hal Rogers) Parkway
London to Hazard. Sources disagree on the opening year: 1971 or 1974.
Green River (Natcher) Parkway
Opened in 1972 (provisional) between Bowling Green and Owensboro.
Cumberland (Louie B. Nunn) Parkway
Opened in 1973 (provisional) from I-65 near Glasgow east to Somerset.
