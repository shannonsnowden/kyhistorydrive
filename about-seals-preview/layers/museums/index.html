Thomas D. Clark Center for Kentucky History

Franklin County · 1999

Flagship Kentucky Historical Society museum, covering more than 12,000 years of the Commonwealth.

Flagship museum of the Kentucky Historical Society covering more than 12,000 years of Kentucky history, with permanent and rotating exhibits plus a major research library. Anchors the Frankfort history campus a short walk from the Old Capitol and Military History Museum. Smithsonian Affiliate and a natural first stop for statewide context.
