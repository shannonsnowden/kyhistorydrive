Cumberland Falls State Resort Park

Whitley County

The “Niagara of the South,” and one of the few Western Hemisphere places a moonbow can appear.

Home of Cumberland Falls—the “Niagara of the South”—and one of the few places in the Western Hemisphere where a moonbow can appear under a full moon. Historic DuPont Lodge and Riverview Restaurant overlook the Cumberland River gorge (lodge dining closed for renovations as of late 2025–2026). Trails, cabins, and falls overlooks remain signature park experiences.
