The Kentucke Gazette

Fayette County · 2014

Kentucky’s first newspaper, begun August 11, 1787 by John and Fielding Bradford.

Weekly Began with Aug. 11, 1787 issue. -v. 2, no. 28 (Mar. 7, 1789). Publishers: John and Fielding Bradford, 1787-1788; John Bradford, 1788-1789. First newspaper published in Kentucky. Cf. Available on microfilm by OmniSys Corp. (for the Library of Virginia).

No. 2 (Aug. 18, 1787). Kentucky gazette (Lexington, Ky. : 1789) 2638-7239 (DLC)sn 82014782 (OCoLC)8796976
