Old Talbott Tavern

Nelson County

Court Square tavern since the late 1700s — among America’s oldest inns, still a working bourbon bar.

Continuously operating since the late 1700s on Bardstown's Court Square—among America's oldest taverns and billed as home to the world's oldest bourbon bar. Southern classics and deep bourbon lists at a single irreplaceable site.
