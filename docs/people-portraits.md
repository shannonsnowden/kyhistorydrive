# People & portraits inventory

Phase 1 of the headshot project: every named historical person in the stories, plus whether a likeness exists that is free to use in articles. Structured data: [`data/people.json`](../data/people.json).

**307 people** · portrait 51 · drawing 5 · photo 14 · doubtful 12 · none-known 225 · free to use and verified: 69

## How this was built

- People were pulled from every `public/content/stories/*.json` body and title (which also covers the names used in the *Related people & stories* data, `public/data/web/related-people.json`), then hand-deduplicated (for example Blue Jacket / Weyapiersenwah, Jacob Beam / Johannes Jacob Boehm, Ann McGinty / Anne Kennedy).
- Modern archaeologists, historians, authors and archivists cited as sources are left out (for example William S. Webb, A. Gwynn Henderson, John Sugden, Michael Veach and Thomas D. Clark). Namesakes and officials who act in a story (such as Louis XVI or Bill Clinton) are included.
- Likenesses were checked against the person's Wikipedia article image and its Wikimedia Commons file page. The license and credit come from the Commons metadata, and every match was reviewed by hand. Event scenes, statues, markers, maps, signatures and graves don't count as likenesses.
- **none-known** means no likeness turned up in this pass. It does not prove none exists. Library of Congress, NPS and Smithsonian searches are still open for the people who matter most.
- **doubtful** marks images that are invented, misattributed or group scenes, including all Native leaders whose online "portraits" can't be traced to a life sitting. Don't use them without a caveat.
- `data/people.json` sits outside `public/` on purpose. It's research and working data that doesn't need to ship. The gallery phase will generate a slim public file (id, name, image, credit) at build time.
