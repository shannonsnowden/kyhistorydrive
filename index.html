Welcome to KY History Drive — a map of Kentucky’s historical markers and a timeline of Kentucky history you can filter by era and year. Start on the map: turn layers on and off in the sidebar, tap a pin for details, and use Full state anytime to zoom back out. Open Timeline to filter by year, turn on layers for places in that range, and read matching stories.

    About

    KY History Drive is a web companion to the KY Markers Drive iPhone app. It helps you explore Kentucky’s historical highway markers on a map, and browse Kentucky history stories on a timeline by era and year.
