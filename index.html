Welcome to KY History Drive — a map of Kentucky’s historical markers, daily history stories, and a timeline you can filter by era and year. Start on the map: turn layers on and off in the sidebar, tap a pin for details, and use Full state anytime to zoom back out. Open Stories to read a story with its place on the map, or Timeline to see what was happening in Kentucky in a chosen timeframe.

    About

    KY History Drive is a web companion to the KY Markers Drive iPhone app. It helps you explore Kentucky’s historical highway markers on a map, read daily Kentucky History stories, and browse those stories on a timeline by era and year.
