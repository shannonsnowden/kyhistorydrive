# Homepage "Route maps" art

The homepage button uses the pill crop. The larger card files stay in this folder and are not on the page.

## Pill (on the page)

Crop of the Warriors' Path card (itself a crop of the Warriors' Path master, Red River crossing to Cumberland Gap). The pill shows the whole route, scaled to the button.

- light and dark: `route-maps-pill-{light,dark}.webp` and `.jpg` at 72×41 (1x), and `@2x` at 144×82.
- Dark is the same crop dimmed so it does not glare on the dark theme.

## Card (not on the page)

Crop of the Warriors' Path master (warriors-path-map-master-3840.png, x1115 y590, 1640x930), Red River crossing to Cumberland Gap. No title or legend.

- light: route-maps-card-light.{webp,jpg} (600x340), @2x (1200x680)
- dark: route-maps-card-dark{,@2x}.{webp,jpg}: same crop dimmed ~30% with a soft edge vignette so it doesn't glare on the dark theme.
- Sizes: 1x webp 38-50 KB; 2x webp 91-119 KB; jpg 49-59 KB (1x), 133-161 KB (2x).

Alt text for the art, not the button name: "Antique parchment map of the Warriors' Path in red, running south through eastern Kentucky past the Kentucky River ford and Flat Lick to Cumberland Gap."
