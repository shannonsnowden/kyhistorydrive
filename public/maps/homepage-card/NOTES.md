# Homepage "Route maps" pill art

Crop of the Warriors' Path card (itself a crop of the Warriors' Path master, Red River crossing to Cumberland Gap). The pill shows the whole route, scaled to the button.

- light and dark: `route-maps-pill-{light,dark}.webp` and `.jpg` at 72×41 (1x), and `@2x` at 144×82.
- Dark is the same crop dimmed so it does not glare on the dark theme.
- The large 600×340 and 1200×680 card files are not on the page.

Alt text for the art, not the button name: "Antique parchment map of the Warriors' Path in red, running south through eastern Kentucky past the Kentucky River ford and Flat Lick to Cumberland Gap."
