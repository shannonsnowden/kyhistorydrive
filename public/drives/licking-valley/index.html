Licking Valley: Stations, Blue Licks and Covered Bridges

Follow the Licking River country from the 1780 British-Indian expedition against Ruddell's and Martin's stations to the 1782 Battle of Blue Licks, with three of the valley's 19th-century covered bridges, ending in old Washington in Mason County.
