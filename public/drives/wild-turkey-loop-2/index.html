    3. Shaker Village of Pleasant Hill

    PublicPaid admissionLimited hoursCovenant 1806. Nonprofit historic site; admission charged, many buildings open seasonally.

    Mercer County converts joined the Shakers in 1805; 44 Believers signed the first family covenant in 1806. As pacifists the Shakers refused to bear arms in the Civil War; the society closed in 1910 and the last Shaker died in 1923. Sources: KHS marker #1481: Shakertown at Pleasant Hill; Shaker Village: History
