Jacob Beam sells Old Jake’s first barrel

In 1795 Jacob Beam (born Johannes Jacob Boehm) sold his first barrel of “Old Jake Beam” sour-mash whiskey from a corn farm and mill on Hardin’s Creek in what was then Washington County, Kentucky.

Photos

Jim Beam White Label

Source: Wikimedia Commons

The family later grew the Old Tub brand; after Prohibition Jim Beam reopened the line, and seven generations still distill from that pioneer start. One early barrel sale became the Beam house that still marks Nelson County’s map.
