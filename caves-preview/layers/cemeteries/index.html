Frankfort Cemetery

Franklin County · 1844

Hilltop rural cemetery (1844) overlooking the capital — Daniel and Rebecca Boone were reinterred here.

Hilltop rural cemetery overlooking the capital, incorporated in 1844 and NRHP-listed (74000872). Daniel and Rebecca Boone were reinterred here in 1845; nineteen Kentucky governors including William Goebel, Vice President Richard Mentor Johnson, and Marine hero Presley O'Bannon also rest on the bluff.
