Abraham Lincoln Birthplace National Historical Park

LaRue County

The Sinking Spring farm where Lincoln was born in 1809, plus the Knob Creek boyhood unit.

Preserves the symbolic birth cabin site and Sinking Spring farm where Abraham Lincoln was born in 1809. The Memorial Building and Knob Creek unit interpret his early Kentucky years. National Historical Park and a cornerstone of Kentucky’s national story.
